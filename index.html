founder · since feb 2026

RangeClick

A game on the live Bitcoin price. Click a price block on the ladder. If the Hyperliquid BTC mark touches it before the 30-second clock runs out, the bet pays its multiplier.

Every accepted bet gets an Ed25519-signed receipt of its terms, so players can check the game without taking the operator's word for it.

  • gameprice ladder beside a 1-second BTC chart; touch or pierce the block within 30, 45 or 60 seconds; 5% edge inside the multiplier
  • fairnesssigned receipts at accept, a per-bet trace from quote to settlement, ledger integrity checks
  • real modenon-custodial RangeVault on Base and Arbitrum Sepolia; the server cannot withdraw player funds or change signed terms

Closed beta. Paper money, $1,000 to start, no deposits. Real-money mode runs on testnets only.
